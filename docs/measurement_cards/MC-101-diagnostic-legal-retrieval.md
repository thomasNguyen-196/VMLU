# MC-101 — Qwen3.5-9B-65K (IEC), Legal MC retrieval test: B / K / O on the 134 wide Legal MC items

Registered 2026-10-29 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. The design is `openspec/changes/diagnostic-legal-retrieval`. It does not change MC-90 to MC-100: their registered text, manifests, rules and analyses stand.

| Why | In MC-100, the provision that governs a Legal MC question (arm O) lifted the items Qwen had missed from 37.0% to 82.6% (+45.7 points, p 0.0004) and kept the items it answered rightly (94.0% against 96.0%); its reading rule was met. Those provisions were located with the gold in view, and in MC-93 three BM25 articles over corpus v0 (arm K) lost points on the Legal MC evidence anchors.
