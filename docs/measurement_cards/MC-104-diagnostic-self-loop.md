# MC-104 — Qwen3.5-9B (IEC) and MiMo V2.6 Flash (OpenCode Go), the self-improvement loop on unused items

Registered 2026-10-30 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. The design is `openspec/changes/diagnostic-router-loop`, decision 11 and task 2.6. It does not change MC-90 to MC-103: their registered text, manifests, rules and analyses stand.

| Field | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Why | **The owner's goal.** The router loop must improve the student model itself, and the student must write every final answer. Escalating unsure items to a stronger model was proposed and rejected, because the result would measure the stronger model.<br>**What is known.** Choosing among B, S and Q per item gained little over a fixed choice per dataset (MC-93 to MC-102). What helped was giving the model what it lacked. On Legal MC, the governing provision lifted Qwen's earlier misses from 37% to 83% (MC-100). On VMLU, step-by-step reasoning (S) lifted the non-thinking Qwen by about 12 points (MC-94, MC-95).<br>**What this card asks.** Does a loop that gives each question the fix it lacks improve the student over its default answer, at what cost, for two different students? |
| Students / routes | **Qwen3.5-9B-65K** (`qwen3-5-9b-65k-prospective`) on the IEC endpoint. Every arm uses the campaign transport of `data/diagnostic_prospective_v1/protocol.json`: Responses API, temperature 1.0, system prompt `Answer the user's question.`, 4,096 output tokens, 300 s timeout, no SDK retries. The exception is the multiple-choice B, which is the MC-43 probe: one chat-completions request with the user prompt only, temperature 0, seed 42, 4 output tokens, and the top 20 log-probabilities of the first token.<br>**MiMo V2.6 Flash** (`mimo-v2.6-flash`, profile `mimo-v2-6-flash`) on the direct OpenCode Go route of MC-103. The settings are those of MC-103: non-streaming chat completions, system prompt `Answer the user's question.`, provider defaults for temperature and reasoning, `max_completion_tokens=20000`, 300 s timeout, no SDK retries, and one routing session header per attempt.<br>Credentials come from `.env` (Qwen) and the local OpenCode auth store (MiMo), and are never written to outputs. |
| Items | 903 items no router has used, the same for both students.<br>• **Knowledge:** 300 ViBidLQA train questions asked without their own passage (dataset `bidlqa-closed-book`). They are the lowest sha256(104, dataset:item) ranks of the 1,128 K2-pool items outside MC-95 and MC-96. The owner chose them as legal knowledge questions; for this study only, that overrides the 2026-10-28 rule that a reading item keeps its passage.<br>• **Multiple choice:** the 303 VMLU dev items, and 300 four-option VM14K items outside MC-95, ranked the same way with seed 104. |
| Arms | **Knowledge, both students:** B (the K2 closed-book prompt: the question only); K with the top 2 clauses of the registered K2 BM25 ranking; K with the top 5 of the same ranking (dataset `bidlqa-closed-book-top5`); O with the item's own clause, a ceiling.<br>**Multiple choice:** S three times (`S-r1` to `S-r3`, the campaign's step-by-step prompt). Qwen answers B once (the probe), MiMo twice (`B-r1`, `B-r2`).<br>**Attempts:** Qwen 3,612; MiMo 4,215.<br>**Files.** Configuration `data/diagnostic_router_loop/self_loop_v1.json`, SHA-256 `110256027249f4f1eb8445c21c0015186f58229755650f666a8100a50c28b354`. Manifests `self_loop_manifest_qwen_v1.json` (self-hash `3138b609e116a149c8f8acb9e1715e19f5c105b003abd772f4638947f3cc4aed`) and `self_loop_manifest_mimo_v1.json` (self-hash `d9488ac1521d4cba230e194a84795ca4c4cd5f843b6131e58412b63a9a85819a`). |
| The loop | The loop is replayed offline from the logged answers; the student writes every final answer.<br>• **Round 1:** B. Qwen is unsure when the probe's confidence (the probability of its letter, renormalized over the offered letters) is below 0.80 or missing. MiMo is unsure when its two B answers differ or one is empty.<br>• **Round 2:** S-r1. Qwen keeps B when S-r1 equals it; MiMo keeps S-r1 when it equals one of its B answers.<br>• **Round 3:** otherwise S-r2 and S-r3 are added and the majority of the three S answers is final. A tie goes to S-r1; an empty answer casts no vote.<br>**The threshold 0.80** comes from a registered rule on design data: the smallest grid value whose two-round loop scores within 1.0 point of always-S on the 660 VMLU valid items of MC-94 and MC-95. On those items it escalates 45.9% and scores 79.39 against 80.15. The inputs' SHA-256 values are in the configuration, and a test reproduces the result.<br>**Missing confidence.** The option block of two VM14K prompts cannot be read by the MC-43 parser, because an option is broken across lines. Their confidence is missing, so the loop treats them as unsure. |
| Scoring | **Multiple choice:** the frozen letter parser; S is scored on the line after the last final-answer label, as in the campaign.<br>**Knowledge:** token-F1 under `vi-reading-v3` (primary; decision 10), with exact match and `vi-reading-v2` beside it. |
| Limits | • **Qwen:** at most 4 requests in flight, one gate shared by the probe and the campaign requests; a breaker after 4 consecutive network failures; the campaign budget guard of 5,000,000 reported tokens.<br>• **MiMo:** at most 4 in flight, the same breaker, and a budget of 6,000,000 reported tokens.<br>• **Halts.** Both halt on an authentication error and on a model-identity drift. For Qwen, the probe accepts the campaign's requested or self-reported id. For MiMo, the id must contain `mimo` and stay the same.<br>• **Resume.** Completed responses, including truncated ones, are terminal; `--resume` re-sends only attempts without one. |
| Gates | Execution requires all of: the `MC-104` heading in `measurement_card.md`; this detail card; `data/diagnostic_router_loop/registration_self_loop.json` with the configuration's SHA-256 and the student's manifest self-hash above; and the student's route (Qwen: the campaign endpoint and model in `.env`; MiMo: the registered host and model). The runner rebuilds the student's manifest from its sources and refuses to run when it differs. |
| Analysis (fixed before data) | `analyze_self_loop`, plan `self-loop-v1`, run once after both runs.<br>• **Multiple choice**, pooled over 603 items, per dataset descriptive. Accuracy and mean reported tokens of always-B, always-S, always-vote (the majority of three S answers), the loop, and the map (MC-95 and MC-103 point maps: Qwen VMLU S, VM14K B; MiMo VMLU B, VM14K S). The loop pays for every request its rounds use. The report also gives the rounds the loop used.<br>• **Knowledge.** Token-F1 and exact match of B, K top 2, K top 5 and O; K as a share of O; scores by where BM25 ranked the own clause (top 2, ranks 3 to 5, below 5).<br>• **Primary tests**, per student:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;1. The loop beats always-B: exact McNemar, two-sided, Holm over the two students. Met when the Holm p is below 0.05 and the loop scores higher.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;2. The loop is not worse than always-S: the mean paired difference with a percentile bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples, seed 7). Met when the lower bound is above -1.0 point and the loop's mean tokens are below always-S's.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;3. K top 5 beats K top 2 on token-F1: sign-flip p (20,000 resamples, seed 7), Holm over the two students. Met when the Holm p is below 0.05 and K top 5 scores higher.<br>The report's top-level keys are recorded in the registration and checked. |
| Provenance and outputs | `condition.json` holds the configuration, manifest, campaign protocol, templates, K2 protocol, retrieval configuration, corpus, excluded-manifest and source hashes, the loop, the student's settings, the route, and the scorer, module, wrapper, engine and runner hashes. Also kept: `attempts.jsonl` (raw replies, usage, and for Qwen's probe the letter log-probabilities), `predictions.jsonl`, `scores.jsonl`, `summary.json`, `run.json` and `operator_events.jsonl`. They sit under `all_res/diagnostic_router_loop/self_loop/<profile>/`. The analysis writes `all_res/diagnostic_router_loop/self_loop/analysis/self_loop_report.json`. Nothing is uploaded. |
| Comparison limits | • **Knowledge framing.** ViBidLQA was built as a reading set: each question was written from its clause, so B without the clause is near zero by design, and BM25 is favoured because questions reuse the clause's words. On these 300 questions BM25 ranks the own clause in the top 2 for 235 and in the top 5 for 268 (from the manifest, before any answer).<br>• **Routes and settings.** The two students run on different routes and settings. Qwen's B is at temperature 0 and its S at temperature 1.0. A difference between the students is therefore also a difference in route and settings.<br>• **Prior exposure.** Both students answered VMLU dev before, in model evaluations: MiMo in MC-59, MC-63, MC-85 and MC-89; Qwen in MC-70, MC-83 and MC-88. None of these designed a router. A model keeps no memory across requests, but exposure in training to these public datasets cannot be measured.<br>• **One draw per policy.** The loop and the fixed policies are compared on the same logged answers; a second run could differ. |
