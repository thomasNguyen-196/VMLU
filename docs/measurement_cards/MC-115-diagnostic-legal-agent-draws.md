# MC-115 — Qwen3.5-9B-65K (IEC) in dsh 0.2.2-rc.1 and omp 18.0.0 over the local folders of legal texts: draws 3 and 4 of DSHF, OMPF and the no-tool baselines (B, C) on MC-114's 646 analysis items (246 MC, 400 closed-book ViBidLQA), each in the condition of its draws 1–2

Registered 2026-11-16 (+07), before any benchmark-item request of this card. The design is in `openspec/changes/diagnostic-legal-agent-draws`. On 2026-11-16 the owner asked to cut the noise in the agent comparisons and chose four draws per item ("mức 4 lượt 1 câu"), that is, two more draws. The owner then approved the design unchanged ("duyệt") with all four defaults: B and C also get draws 3–4; the primary tests use 4-draw means; the 2.24-point margin applies to M1–M3; a 4-attempt smoke test runs before registration. This card does not change MC-90 to MC-114: their registered text, manifests, rules, analyses and conclusions stand. MC-112's, MC-113's and MC-114's modules are imported, not edited.
