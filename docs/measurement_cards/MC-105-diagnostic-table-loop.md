# MC-105 — Qwen3.5-9B (IEC) and MiMo V2.6 Flash (OpenCode Go), the self-improvement loop on the /table suite, before and after

Registered 2026-10-31 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. The design is `openspec/changes/diagnostic-router-loop`, decision 12 and task 2.7. It does not change MC-90 to MC-104: their registered text, manifests, rules and analyses stand.

| Field | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Why | **The owner's request.** Compare before and after on the datasets /table shows, so the loop's effect reads against the familiar benchmark numbers.<br>**What is known.** On held-out items, the loop of MC-104 lifted Qwen's multiple choice from 69.0% to 76.3% over its own direct answer (Holm p below 0.0001). For MiMo the gain was 2.2 points and not significant.<br>**What this card asks.** What the same loop, with the student writing every final answer, does to each student's numbers on the six /table datasets with local gold. |
| Students / routes | As MC-104.<br>• **Qwen3.5-9B-65K** (`qwen3-5-9b-65k-prospective`) on the IEC endpoint with the campaign transport of `data/diagnostic_prospective_v1/protocol.json`: Responses API, temperature 1.0, system prompt `Answer the user's question.`, 4,096 output tokens, 300 s timeout, no SDK retries. The exception is the multiple-choice B-r1, which is the MC-43 probe: chat completions with the user prompt only, temperature 0, seed 42, 4 output tokens, and the top 20 log-probabilities of the first token.<br>• **MiMo V2.6 Flash** (`mimo-v2.6-flash`) on the direct OpenCode Go route of MC-103: non-streaming chat completions, system prompt `Answer the user's question.`, provider defaults for temperature and reasoning, `max_completion_tokens=20000`, 300 s timeout, no SDK retries, one routing session header per attempt.<br>Credentials come from `.env` (Qwen) and the local OpenCode auth store (MiMo), and are never written to outputs. |
| Items | The 2,043 items of the six main-v1 datasets with local gold, with the items and prompts of the /table runs: VMLU valid 744, Legal MC 146, Legal NLI 150 (two options, Có and Không), Vi-SQuAD 200, Vi-DROP 200 and ViBidLQA test 603. Every item is checked row by row, prompt included, against the frozen main-v1 manifest (`data/vi_multimodel_v1/main_v1.json`, self-hash `0d413341f1fceea2e131e3a7fe7b73032e31303c8935ce5fba93c5f40b126551`).<br>• **VMLU dev (303)** is MC-104's: the same loop and students. It is reported beside the six datasets, not re-run.<br>• **VMLU test** is not run, because only vmlu.ai scores it.<br>• **Seen before.** Earlier router cards used 660 of the 744 VMLU valid items (MC-94, MC-95); they include the 660 design items of the 0.80 threshold. In the other five datasets, MC-90 to MC-92 used 1,239 items and MC-93 the other 60. |
| Arms | **B:** the frozen main-v1 prompt, byte for byte the /table prompt.<br>**S:** the campaign step-by-step template of `data/diagnostic_prospective_v1/prompt_templates_v1.json`, as in MC-90 to MC-104.<br>**Multiple choice:** Qwen answers B once (`B-r1`, the probe) and MiMo twice (`B-r1`, `B-r2`); both answer S three times (`S-r1` to `S-r3`).<br>**Reading:** both students answer B twice and S three times.<br>**Attempts:** Qwen 9,175; MiMo 10,215.<br>**Files.** Configuration `data/diagnostic_router_loop/table_loop_v1.json`, SHA-256 `1c955756db4f26c70f46953e98c612ed0fc0028cf33e5a414776f8cc47cb2611`. Manifests `table_loop_manifest_qwen_v1.json` (self-hash `5a4e4e76c3c3949a898edf7b81e75a2d4f54f0ff6ff8ccf95b41668ba81cb9b0`) and `table_loop_manifest_mimo_v1.json` (self-hash `b9fddd9480db40e56bd54a3a8fd7eac2b969d9254e6e6aab86db6c3531644f3b`). |
| The loop | The loop is replayed offline from the logged answers; the final answer is always one of the student's own logged answers.<br>• **Multiple choice:** MC-104's rules, unchanged. Qwen keeps B-r1 when its probe confidence is at least 0.80. Otherwise it keeps B-r1 when S-r1 agrees with it, and else takes the majority of S-r1 to S-r3. MiMo keeps B-r1 when its two B answers agree. Otherwise it keeps S-r1 when S-r1 agrees with one of them, and else takes the majority. The 0.80 threshold is MC-104's, and it applies unchanged to the two-option Legal NLI.<br>• **Reading, both students:** keep B-r1 when B-r1 and B-r2 agree. Otherwise keep S-r1 when S-r1 agrees with one of them. Otherwise take the majority of S-r1 to S-r3 by normalized answer: a tie goes to the earliest, the earliest attempt with the winning answer is final, an empty answer casts no vote, and with no answer S-r1 is final.<br>• **Agreement:** router v2's normalized-answer-v1 (MC-94). Both parsed answers must be non-empty and equal. Multiple choice compares the letter; reading compares the exact-match normalization of the vi-reading-v3 prediction. |
| Scoring | **Multiple choice:** the frozen letter parser; S is scored on the line after the last final-answer label, as in the campaign.<br>**Reading:** token-F1 under `vi-reading-v3` (primary; decision 10), with exact match beside it and `vi-reading-v2` beside both. |
| Limits | • **Qwen:** at most 4 requests in flight, one gate shared by the probe and the campaign requests; a breaker after 4 consecutive network failures; a budget guard of 10,000,000 reported tokens. About 7.6 million are projected from MC-90 and MC-94 on the same datasets.<br>• **MiMo:** at most 6 in flight (MC-104: 4), to finish in about 10 hours; MC-85 ran MiMo on these datasets with 12 workers. The same breaker applies, and the budget guard is 10,000,000 reported tokens. About 7.6 million are projected, about USD 1.8 at list price.<br>• **Halts.** Both halt on an authentication error and on a model-identity drift, as in MC-104.<br>• **Resume.** Completed responses, including truncated ones, are terminal; `--resume` re-sends only attempts without one. |
| Gates | Execution requires all of: the `MC-105` heading in `measurement_card.md`; this detail card; `data/diagnostic_router_loop/registration_table_loop.json` with the configuration's SHA-256 and the student's manifest self-hash above; and the student's route (Qwen: the campaign endpoint and model in `.env`; MiMo: the registered host and model). The runner rebuilds the student's manifest, re-checking every item against the main-v1 manifest, and refuses to run when it differs. |
| Analysis (fixed before data) | `analyze_table_loop`, plan `table-loop-v1`, run once after both runs.<br>• **Per student and dataset:** always-B (B-r1, the before), always-S (S-r1), always-vote (the majority of three S answers) and the loop (the after), each with its score and mean reported tokens; also the rounds the loop used. The loop pays for every request its rounds use. Two B answers count in round 1, except Qwen's multiple choice.<br>• **Primary test:** the loop against always-B in each of the 12 student and dataset cells. Multiple choice uses an exact two-sided McNemar test. Reading uses the mean paired token-F1 difference with a two-sided sign-flip p-value (20,000 resamples, seed 7). Holm corrects over the 12. A cell is met when its Holm p is below 0.05 and the loop scores higher.<br>• **Descriptive:**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– loop minus always-S, and always-vote minus always-S, as mean paired differences with a percentile bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples, seed 7);<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– the number /table publishes beside each dataset (MC-83 for Qwen, MC-85 for MiMo, from `web/data/benchmark-reports.json`, whose SHA-256 the report records);<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– MC-104's VMLU dev row beside the /table VMLU dev number.<br>The report's top-level keys are recorded in the registration and checked. |
| Provenance and outputs | `condition.json` holds the configuration, manifest, main-v1 manifest, campaign protocol, templates, earlier-manifest and source hashes, the loop, the student's settings, the route, and the scorer, module, wrapper, engine, shared runner (`run_self_loop`) and runner hashes. Also kept: `attempts.jsonl` (raw replies, usage, and for Qwen's probe the letter log-probabilities), `predictions.jsonl`, `scores.jsonl`, `summary.json`, `run.json` and `operator_events.jsonl`. They sit under `all_res/diagnostic_router_loop/table_loop/<profile>/`. The analysis writes `all_res/diagnostic_router_loop/table_loop/analysis/table_loop_report.json`. Nothing is uploaded. After the run, the results are published on /table beside MC-83 and MC-85. |
| Comparison limits | • **Not held-out.** See Items: the before and after describe the benchmark numbers; the held-out evidence is MC-104.<br>• **Before.** The before is the within-run B. The /table numbers come from other routes and settings. MC-83 allowed 20,000 output tokens and sent no temperature. MC-85 used the Responses API with a 20,000-token cap.<br>• **Qwen's two befores.** Qwen's multiple-choice B is the probe at temperature 0 with 4 output tokens; its reading B is a campaign request at temperature 1.0.<br>• **Prior exposure.** Both students answered these items before, in model evaluations (MiMo: MC-54, MC-63, MC-85, MC-89; Qwen: MC-56, MC-70, MC-72, MC-83, MC-88), and Qwen also in MC-90 to MC-95. A model keeps no memory across requests, but exposure in training to these public datasets cannot be measured.<br>• **One draw per policy.** The policies are compared on the same logged answers; a second run could differ. |

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-01, decided by the owner before the analysis was run; the registered text above is unchanged)

| Field | Amendment |
| --- | --- |
| What happened | Two MiMo attempts never returned: B-r1 and B-r2 of VMLU valid item `04-0018`. Both timed out at the registered 300 s in all five sends: the main pass, a resume, and three timeout re-sends. On this item MiMo reasons up to its 20,000-token cap, and the S-r1 that did complete took 299.5 s. Every other attempt of both students has a completed response (Qwen 9,175 of 9,175; MiMo 10,213 of 10,215). |
| Gap in the registration | The card says how a completed response is scored (a truncated or empty one scores as no answer), but not how to score an attempt that never returns. |
| Decision | The two attempts are scored as no answer: an empty prediction, incorrect, with unknown usage. The loop treats them as it treats any empty answer: they never agree and cast no vote. The item stays in the denominator, so MiMo's VMLU valid keeps its 744 items; the largest possible effect on a score is 0.13 point. The alternatives offered were to drop the item from MiMo's VMLU valid, or to keep re-sending at the same 300 s timeout. |
| Implementation | `data/diagnostic_router_loop/registration_table_loop.json` lists the two run keys under `amendments`. `analyze_table_loop.load_rows` accepts a run that stops short by exactly those attempts and adds each as a no-answer row. It fails closed when any other attempt is missing or the run's error count differs. `test_analyze_table_loop` covers both cases. |
| Timing | Decided and committed after the last re-send and before the registered analysis was run; no score of this card had been computed. |
