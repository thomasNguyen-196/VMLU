# MC-111 — Qwen3.5-9B-65K (IEC), Legal MC "select, then answer": B / K / K10 / SEL / O on the 134 wide Legal MC items

Registered 2026-11-09 (+07), before any benchmark-item request of this card. The design is in `openspec/changes/diagnostic-legal-select`, which the owner approved unchanged on 2026-11-08. This card does not change MC-90 to MC-110: their registered text, manifests, rules and analyses stand.

| Why | **MC-101.** BM25's top 2 units (arm K) scored 84.7, against 84.3 for B, so its reading rule was not met. K kept 38% of the gain that MC-100's oracle provisions (arm O) brought on the items Qwen had missed.<br>**Post hoc pre-check** (`code_benchmark/analyze_legal_retrieval_misses.py`, no requests), on the 53 items with oracle provisions:<br>• BM25's top 2 hold an oracle article for 40 items.<br>• The 13 misses: 1 item has no oracle document in the corpus, 3 have another article of an oracle document in the top 2, and 9 have only other documents there.<br>• BM25's top 10 hold an oracle article for 47 of the 53 items.<br>The missing filter is therefore mainly an ordering problem among units the search already found. This card asks whether Qwen itself, shown BM25's top 10, selects the governing article better than BM25's order (the filter), and whether answering from its own selection beats the closed-book prompt on the pool (the route). |

| Analysis (fixed before data) | `analyze_legal_select`, plan `diagnostic-legal-select-v1`, run once after both rounds.<br>**Item score.** The mean over the two draws: correctness for an answer arm (0, 0.5 or 1), the selection hit for PICK.<br>**Selection hit.** The selection holds an oracle article. Matching is by document number (NFC, case, spaces and Đ/D folded) and article number, with any part counting: the matching of MC-101's hit report. An empty or unparsed selection is a miss.<br>**Question 1, filter.** On the 53 oracle items: PICK's selection hit against BM25's top-2 hit (0 or 1 per item, 40 of 53 overall).<br>**Question 2, route.** On the 134 items: always-SEL against always-B.<br>**Statistics.** For each question: the mean per-item difference, a 95% percentile bootstrap (2,000 resamples, seed 7) and a two-sided paired sign-flip p-value (20,000 resamples, seed 7). Holm is applied over the two p-values at α = 0.05.<br>**Reading rule.**<br>• Filter met when PICK's mean exceeds BM25's and its adjusted p is below α: Qwen filters retrieved evidence better than BM25's order.<br>• Filter worse when PICK's mean is below BM25's and its adjusted p is below α.<br>• Route met when SEL's mean exceeds B's and its adjusted p is below α: SEL should be the Legal MC route. Otherwise always-B stays the Legal MC route.<br>**Description.**<br>• **Per stratum.** On the `supports_gold` items of each MC-100 stratum and on the 78 items outside MC-100: B, K, K10, SEL and O, with SEL−K, SEL−K10, K10−K and SEL−O. In `wrong`, the share of O's gain kept, (arm − B)/(O − B), for K, K10 and SEL.<br>• **Selection behaviour.** How often 0, 1 or 2 units are selected, and how many selections are unparsed. The positions chosen, including the share of selections that are exactly [1, 2]. Precision: the share of selected units that are oracle articles. Recall within reach: hits on the 47 items whose top 10 hold an oracle article. `Không có` on the 6 oracle items whose top 10 hold none.<br>• **By selection.** SEL's accuracy when its selection hits and when it misses.<br>• **Gold check.** Each arm's options on the `contradicts_gold` items (the gold's, the owner's, other), read from MC-100's owner review by its registered SHA-256.<br>• **Pool.** Clear rescues and regressions; the share answering A; mean reported tokens per arm, with SEL counting both steps.<br>• **Replication.** This run's B, K and O beside MC-101's (pool B 84.33, pool K 84.70, O on `wrong` 84.78).<br>The report's top-level keys are recorded in the registration and checked. |
