# MC-109 — Qwen3.5-9B (IEC), the self-improvement loop on VMLU test, asked round by round

Registered 2026-11-05 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. The design is `openspec/changes/diagnostic-router-loop`, task 2.9. It does not change MC-90 to MC-108: their registered text, manifests, rules and analyses stand.

| Why | **The owner's request.** Fill the VMLU Test column of the /table before-and-after table (`docs/router-before-after/README.md`) for Qwen, starting with Qwen at 6 requests in flight; MiMo later, under its own card.<br>**What is known.** On VMLU valid, MC-105's loop lifted Qwen from 69.9% (its within-run probe) to 82.0% (Holm p below 0.001). | The loop | MC-104's and MC-105's rule for Qwen's multiple choice, unchanged:<br>• **Round 1.** Keep B-r1 when its probe confidence is at least 0.80.<br>• **Round 2.** Otherwise keep B-r1 when S-r1 agrees with it.<br>• **Round 3.** Otherwise take the majority of S-r1 to S-r3. A tie goes to the earliest; an empty answer casts no vote; with no answer, S-r1 is final.<br>• **Agreement:** router v2's normalized-answer-v1. Both parsed letters must be non-empty and equal.<br>The final answer is always one of Qwen's own logged answers. |
| Schedule (what differs from MC-105) | MC-105 asked every attempt and replayed the rule offline. Here **each attempt is asked only when the rule needs it**:<br>• the probe for every item;<br>• S-r1 only when the confidence is below 0.80 or missing;<br>• S-r2 and S-r3 only when S-r1 does not agree with B-r1.<br>The attempts are independent requests, so the final answer is the one MC-105's replay (`diagnostic_table_loop.choose`) would choose from the same attempts; the tests check this on 3,000 random cases. The attempts the rule never needs are not sent, so there is no always-S or always-vote policy here.<br>**Passes.** Each pass sends every attempt the rule needs, given the completed attempts so far. An attempt without a completed response (a technical failure, not a fatal one) is sent again in the next pass. A launch stops after 6 passes, after a pass that completes nothing, or at a fatal row; `--resume` continues from the ledger. No pass sends one attempt twice or re-sends a completed one: the runner fails closed if it would, the engine hands each attempt to exactly one lane under a lock, and one output admits one launch at a time (file lock). |
| Scoring | The frozen letter parser. B-r1 is parsed from the probe's reply; S is parsed from the line after the last final-answer label, as in the campaign. A truncated or unparsed reply is an empty answer. |
| Limits | • **In flight:** at most 6 requests, the owner's choice on 2026-11-05 (MC-105: 4), behind one gate shared by the probe and the S requests.<br>• **Breaker:** after 4 consecutive network failures.<br>• **Budget guard:** 20,000,000 reported tokens. Projected from MC-105's VMLU valid rounds and usage: about 1.2 million for the probes and about 9–11 million for S. Expected duration at 6 in flight: about 1 hour of probes and 9–15 hours of S.<br>• **Halts.** On an authentication error, a model-identity drift, the budget or the breaker, as in MC-105.<br>• **Resume.** Completed responses, including truncated ones, are terminal. |
| Gates | Execution requires all of: the `MC-109` heading in `measurement_card.md`; this detail card; `data/diagnostic_router_loop/registration_vmlu_test_loop.json` with the configuration's SHA-256 and the manifest self-hash above; and the campaign endpoint and model in `.env`. |
| Analysis (fixed before data) | Plan `vmlu-test-loop-v1`.<br>• **Outputs.** After each pass the runner writes `loop.jsonl` and `summary.json`. Per item, `loop.jsonl` holds the attempts the rule read, their letters, the probe confidence, the rounds and the final answer. Once every item has a final answer, the runner also writes `submission_before.csv` (B-r1) and `submission_loop.csv` (the loop), both `id,answer` for vmlu.ai.<br>• **Scores.** The owner submits the two files to vmlu.ai, and its reported accuracy for each is entered in the run record.<br>• **Primary.** The loop's accuracy minus the before's, descriptive. | Provenance and outputs | `condition.json` holds:<br>• the configuration, manifest, v2 VMLU manifest, campaign protocol, template and source hashes;<br>• the schedule, the loop, the student's settings and the route;<br>• the module, loop-replay, router, wrapper, engine, base engine, shared runner and runner hashes.<br>Also kept: `attempts.jsonl` (raw replies, usage, and the probe's letter log-probabilities), `predictions.jsonl`, `loop.jsonl`, `summary.json`, `run.json`, `source_manifest.json` and `operator_events.jsonl`. | Comparison limits | • **Before.** The before is the within-run probe at temperature 0 with 4 output tokens. The /table numbers come from other routes and settings (MC-83: Responses API, temperature 1.0, 20,000 tokens; MC-70: OMP without tools).<br>• **One draw.** A second run could differ.<br>• **Prior exposure.** Qwen answered these items before (MC-70, MC-83).
