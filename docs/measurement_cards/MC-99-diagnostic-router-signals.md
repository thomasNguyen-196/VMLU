# MC-99 — Jev 1.13 Free (OpenCode console), router signals: pre-hoc question signals and post-hoc answer checks on the 967 wide reading items, plus an offline logprob cascade on VMLU

Registered 2026-10-28 (+07) before any benchmark-item request of this card. The design is `openspec/changes/diagnostic-router-signals`. It does not change MC-90 to MC-98: their registered text, manifests, rules, procedures and analyses stand.

| Field | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Why | MC-98 stopped the Muse router at the design stage: on ViBidLQA, 66.16 token-F1 for Muse, 66.25 for keywords, 65.88 for always-S.<br>After that, the project owner asked for two things:<br>• try a second router model, Jev 1.13;<br>• study the router so that its conclusions do not depend on the model that sorts the questions.<br>An exploratory offline analysis of the existing draws, run before this card, found three things:<br>• Per-item benefits repeat weakly between draws: the correlation of S − B is 0.21 on ViBidLQA.<br>• The repeatable part runs both ways. On ViBidLQA, S wins both draws on 99 items and B on 74.<br>• An ideal router could gain up to 6.6 points over always-S there; this cap comes from the mean and variance alone. The routers so far gained 0.3 to 1.0.<br>So the router line is limited by its signals, not by a ceiling. This card measures two new kinds of signal:<br>• pre-hoc signals from the passage and question;<br>• post-hoc checks of the answer B actually gave.<br>It also measures the student model's own letter confidence. Every signal is compared on one footing. |
| Scope | The 967 wide reading items of the menu set (`data/diagnostic_llm_router/menu_q_v1.json`): ViBidLQA test 591, Vi-DROP 188, Vi-SQuAD 188. Passage and question come from each item's frozen reading prompt.<br>**J1:** one pre-hoc request per item.<br>**J2:** two post-hoc requests per item:<br>• one with the scored B-r1 prediction of MC-90;<br>• one with the scored B-r2 prediction of MC-91.<br>2,901 requests in all. Manifest `data/diagnostic_router_signals/jev_signals_manifest_v1.json`, self-hash `a96473956a1f4f36be50912b9205ae6d75730f8cce38bc11a9ee6ebd9f053f16`, one row per request with the SHA-256 of its body.<br>No student-model request is sent. The student draws are those of MC-90 to MC-92 and MC-97.<br>**J3** is offline: 660 VMLU items, of which 300 are in MC-94 and 360 in MC-95. Each has B and S twice, and the MC-43/44 temperature-0 answer with its first-token letter confidence. |
| Requests | Configuration `data/diagnostic_router_signals/jev_signals_v1.json`, SHA-256 `5804fcb6156aa916e0108bc63906ebecbf62c5640a1e34a77c57a6a43235a805` (`jev-signals-v1`).<br>The state is a JSON object: the passage and question, plus `proposed_answer` for J2. Instructions and option descriptions are in English, because Jev's documentation gives English as its primary training language. The state stays in Vietnamese.<br>**J1:** a Choice `group` over the six MC-98 groups, with their definitions translated, and seven yes/no questions:<br>• `asks_designation`;<br>• `needs_computation`;<br>• `asks_whole_list`;<br>• `asks_one_part`;<br>• `asks_condition`;<br>• `verbatim_short`;<br>• `passage_insufficient`.<br>**J2:** four yes/no questions about the proposed answer:<br>• `answer_correct`;<br>• `answer_complete`;<br>• `answer_extra`;<br>• `answer_verbatim`.<br>No request carries the gold answer, a score, an audit field or another arm's output. |
| Route and credential | Model `jev-1.13-free` on the OpenCode console System One endpoint: host `opencode.ai`, path `/inference/systemone/v1/systemone`.<br>The credential is the OpenCode key of the local auth store. It is resolved in the runner process and never written to outputs.<br>Each request carries an `x-opencode-session` header derived from its run key, and the user agent `vmlu-diagnostic/1.0`.<br>Two non-benchmark probes on 2026-10-28 returned a choice with probabilities and a noul value at zero cost (`billing_source: free`). |
| Limits and halts | 2 requests in flight and a 120 s timeout.<br>A 429 or 5xx response is re-sent after its Retry-After wait (30 s without one), at most 5 times; each retry is recorded.<br>The run halts on any of these:<br>• a response with a non-zero cost or a billing source other than `free`;<br>• a response model other than `jev-1.13-free`;<br>• an authentication error (401 or 403);<br>• 4 consecutive network failures.<br>A worker that receives a halting response stops further sends at once. Completed responses are terminal. `--resume` re-sends only requests without a completed response, under an identical frozen condition. |
| Analysis (fixed before data) | Plan `diagnostic-router-signals-v1`, `python -m code_benchmark.analyze_router_signals --run-dir <output dir>`, run once. It reports:<br>• **The ceiling block.** Per dataset and arm pair: the draw correlation of the per-item benefit, items won on both draws, the gain of a single-draw router, the distribution-free cap and the normal-model estimate of an ideal router's gain. It covers reading (S − B, Q − B, Q − S), VMLU (S − B) and MC-96 (K − B, KS − K, O − K).<br>• **Reading pre-hoc policies.** Each chooses on one draw and is scored on the other, both ways, averaged:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P1, the best fixed arm per dataset, is the baseline;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P2 keyword groups;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P3 Muse groups (MC-98, ViBidLQA);<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P4 Jev groups;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P5 Jev groups gated at confidence 0.5;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P6 a ridge rule (λ = 1) on Jev's probabilities, B or S, fitted on 5 passage-grouped folds.<br>• **Reading post-hoc policies.** Each decides from the answer B gave on a draw and is scored on that draw:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P7, agreement of the two B answers;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P8, the Jev answer check at 0.5;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P9, the same check with its threshold chosen on the other draw;<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– P10, a combined check.<br>• **VMLU.** M2, always-S, is the baseline; M1 is always-B at temperature 0; M3 is the agreement of the two B answers; M4 is the logprob cascade, its threshold chosen on the other draw (one item without a confidence counts as 0).<br>• **For every policy:** the score, mean student-model tokens and the escalated share. Against the baseline: the per-item difference with a bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples, seed 7) and a sign-flip p-value (20,000 resamples, seed 7).<br>• Jev's spread and its agreement with Muse and the keywords, and how well its answer check tracks B's token-F1 (Spearman, AUROC).<br>**Reading rule.** A policy is promising when, against its baseline on the same items, either:<br>• its pooled score is at least 1.0 point higher; or<br>• it is at most 0.5 points lower at no more than 60% of the baseline's mean student-model tokens.<br>Only a promising policy goes to a held-out card. |
| Gates | Execution requires all of:<br>• the `MC-99` heading in `measurement_card.md`;<br>• this detail card;<br>• `data/diagnostic_router_signals/registration.json`, with the configuration and manifest hashes above and the registered route;<br>• a credential;<br>• a manifest that the runner rebuilds identically from the frozen items, the configuration and the registered answer sources. |
| Provenance and outputs | Everything is under `all_res/diagnostic_router_signals/jev/jev-1.13-free/`, with no upload:<br>• `condition.json`: the configuration, manifest, answer-source, card, module and runner hashes; the route; the credential source label, never the key; the limits;<br>• `attempts.jsonl`: per request, the answers with probabilities, usage with cost and billing source, the response model, latency and retries;<br>• `signals.jsonl` and `run.json`;<br>• `operator_events.jsonl`, one line per launch. |
| Comparison limits | • The items are design data. Every result is design-stage, and the ceiling block was first computed exploratorily before this card.<br>• Jev's accuracy on Vietnamese is documented as lower than on English. A flat signal may reflect the language rather than the method. The report shows how spread Jev's probabilities are.<br>• J3 sets a temperature-0 answer and confidence from MC-43/44 against temperature-1.0 S draws from other runs.<br>• Token-F1 compares words, not meaning.<br>• Jev's tokens are another model's tokens. They carry no cost here and are reported apart. |
