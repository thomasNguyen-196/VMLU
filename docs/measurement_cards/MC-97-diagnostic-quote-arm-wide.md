# MC-97 — Qwen3.5-9B-65K (IEC), LLM router design run: arm Q ("trích rồi đáp") on the 967 wide reading items

Registered 2026-10-28 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. The design is `openspec/changes/diagnostic-llm-router`. It does not change MC-90 to MC-96: their registered text, manifests, rules, procedures and analyses stand.

| Field | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Why | On 2026-10-28 the project owner set what the router is.<br>• The baseline of a reading item is its passage and question.<br>• The router is an LLM that reads the passage and the question and tells the student model how to work on that question.<br>• The router's groups come from experiments on this model.<br>The wide draws of MC-90 to MC-92 measure what B and S alone leave for such a router on the 967 reading items (token-F1):<br>• always-B 68.1 and always-S 72.4;<br>• the best fixed arm per dataset 73.1;<br>• choosing per question from draw 1 and scoring draw 2, 73.7.<br>The winner of draw 1 wins again on draw 2 for 61% of ViBidLQA items, against 50% by chance.<br>On ViBidLQA, the largest loss with a visible pattern is over-long answers, which contain the gold answer and more: 24% to 27% of attempts and 10 to 12 token-F1 points.<br>Arm Q targets that loss. The owner chose:<br>• B, S and Q as the menu;<br>• Muse Spark 1.3 as the router model;<br>• a router that returns one group name.<br>This card runs Q on the design items. The router itself is registered on a later card. |
| Scope | Every wide-phase item of ViBidLQA test (591), Vi-DROP (188) and Vi-SQuAD (188): the B-r1 rows of `data/diagnostic_prospective_v1/wide_v1.json`, in manifest order, 967 items. Arm Q, two draws each, 1,934 attempts. B (MC-90 B-r1, MC-91 B-r2) and S (MC-90 S-r1, MC-92 S-r2) already have two draws on the same items. Legal MC and Legal NLI are not run, because Q asks for a span answer. |
| Prompt | Q is the protocol's head, then the passage and the question of the item's frozen main-v1 reading prompt, in the layout of the S reading prompt: head + passage + `\n\nCâu hỏi: ` + question + `\n`. The head reads, followed by a blank line:<br>`` Đọc đoạn văn dưới đây và trả lời câu hỏi. Trước hết, chép nguyên văn câu hoặc vế câu trong đoạn văn trả lời trực tiếp câu hỏi vào một dòng có dạng `Trích dẫn: <phần chép nguyên văn>`. Sau đó, từ phần trích dẫn, lấy ra cụm từ hoặc số ngắn nhất trả lời đúng điều được hỏi; không thêm tên văn bản, số điều, số khoản hay lời giải thích nếu câu hỏi không hỏi tới. Dòng cuối cùng phải đúng một dòng có dạng `Trả lời: <cụm từ hoặc số đó>`, không dùng định dạng markdown và không viết thêm gì sau dòng đó. ``<br>Each item's frozen reading prompt must hash to its MC-90 B prompt. No prompt carries the gold answer. |
| Protocol and inputs | `data/diagnostic_llm_router/protocol.json`, SHA-256 `0b9dc30eeb15a24d8004cc4f3c6c8380d016d9e53fa9a801b263133b15261866`. | Model / route / connectivity | As MC-90 and MC-93 to MC-96 (see their cards): Qwen3.5-9B-65K served by the IEC internal gateway through the openvpn3 `member-shared-tcp` tunnel, direct API, OpenAI Responses API with streaming. The endpoint host is resolved inside the runner process through the tunnel DNS. The endpoint self-reports `/home/llm/llama.cpp/models/Qwen3.5-9B-Q4_K_M.gguf`; a change of that id pauses the run. |
| Sampling and limits | As MC-96: temperature 1.0 requested, effective value not observable; seed and reasoning field omitted; system prompt `Answer the user's question.`; `max_output_tokens=4096`; 300 s timeout; no SDK retries. Budget guard 5,000,000 reported tokens for this run (expected about 1.6 million). |
| Concurrency | Engine of 12 lanes (4 shards x 3 lanes) with at most 4 requests in flight and a breaker that pauses the run after 4 consecutive network failures. These limits are registered in `data/diagnostic_llm_router/registration.json` and applied by default; other values are refused. |
| Retries | No in-run retry of a failed item. `--resume` re-attempts only items without a completed response under an identical frozen condition; completed responses, including truncated or unparsed ones, are terminal. |
| Scoring | As S on reading in MC-90 to MC-96: Q is scored from the text after the last final-answer label (`scoring_source` `raw_last_label`) with the frozen V1 reading scorer, so the `Trích dẫn:` line is not scored. Exact match and token-F1 are kept, with the format flags of S (a final-answer label present, markdown on the final line). Unparsed answers stay in denominators. |
| Gates | Execution requires all of: the `MC-97` heading in `measurement_card.md`; this detail card; the registration with the menu protocol, campaign protocol, wide manifest and menu manifest hashes above; and the endpoint host and model of the campaign. |
| Analysis (fixed before data) | Plan `diagnostic-menu-q-v1`. Descriptive, with no confirmatory test. On the 967 items, with B and S from MC-90 to MC-92, it reports:<br>• per dataset and arm, mean token-F1 and exact match over the two draws, and mean reported tokens per attempt;<br>• per item, the arm with the highest mean token-F1, and whether the winner of draw 1 wins again on draw 2;<br>• two bounds for choosing per item: choose on draw 1 and score draw 2 (and the reverse), and choose and score on the same draws;<br>• the best fixed arm per dataset;<br>• each arm's failure types (over-long, partial, near, wrong, mixed) and the token-F1 each loses.<br>The router's groups, its prompt and the mapping from group to arm are written from these draws. They are registered on a later card before any router request. |
| Provenance and outputs | `condition.json` (manifest, protocols, wide manifest, menu module, runner, engine, wrapper, scorer and card hashes, and the operational limits), per-attempt usage and response model id, and `operator_events.jsonl` for every launch, all under `all_res/diagnostic_llm_router/menu/qwen3-5-9b-65k-prospective/`; no upload. |
| Comparison limits | • B and S come from runs of 2026-10-22, and Q runs on 2026-10-28 or later on the same endpoint and model id, so an endpoint drift would bias Q against B and S. MC-96's O repeated MC-95's ViBidLQA B condition without a sign of drift (67.6 against 64.7 token-F1 on other items).<br>• The design items also served the design of routers v1 to v3. They are design data, not held-out data.<br>• Q asks for the shortest phrase, so it may cut answers that need several items, and it may suit Vi-DROP questions that need a calculation less well than S.<br>• Token-F1 and exact match compare words, not meaning; Q's gains or losses may partly be measurement effects, as MC-94's semantic review found for EM.<br>• Single draws at temperature 1.0 are noisy, which is why every arm has two. |
