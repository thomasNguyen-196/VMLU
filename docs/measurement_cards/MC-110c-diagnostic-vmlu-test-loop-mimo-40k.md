# MC-110c — MiMo V2.6 Flash (OpenCode Go), MC-110's unanswered attempts asked again with a 40,000-token cap

Registered 2026-11-10 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. It is a recovery of MC-110, a separate condition. It reuses MC-110b's replies that stopped below the 20,000-token cap. It does not change MC-110 or MC-110b: their registered text, outputs and submissions stand, and MC-110's result stays MC-110's result.

During its first pass, 77 of its 94 replies were cut by the cap again with no visible answer.<br>The owner then asked for a 40,000-token cap ("tăng trần lên 40k"), and chose the wider scope: every MC-110 attempt left without an answer by a timeout or by the cap.<br>**What is known.**<br>• MC-110 had 538 attempts without an answer for these two causes, on 154 items:<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– 183 timed out three times at 300 s (MC-110's amendment 2);<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– 355 were cut at 20,000 completion tokens before any visible text (finish reason `length`); none of the cut replies had a parsable letter.<br>• OpenCode Go lists `mimo-v2.6-flash` with 131,072 output tokens, so 40,000 is within the model's limit.<br>• At 20,000 tokens a cut reply took about 450–650 s.
