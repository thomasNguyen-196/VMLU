# MC-93 — Qwen3.5-9B-65K (IEC), diagnostic prospective core: B / S / O / K / KS on the 60 audit anchors, router v1

Registered 2026-10-27 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. Successor of the offline audit in `diagnostic-protocol-ledger-pilot`; the design is `openspec/changes/diagnostic-prospective-campaign` (Decisions 11 and 12). The wide phase (MC-90, with the supplements MC-91 and MC-92) is finished and is not changed by this card.

| Why | The single evaluation of the frozen router v1 and the arm comparisons on the 60 audited anchors: does routing by need beat answering every item one way, at what token cost, and do the arms recover the failures the audit attributed to reasoning, reading or missing legal evidence. |

| Scope | The 60 ordered anchors of the locked pilot (pilot content SHA-256 `19b31d71643d8c894edb0afe3439fc35270e51099c51e49bbde1fe9b0d774b26`): 12 each of Legal MC, Legal NLI, Vi-SQuAD, Vi-DROP and ViBidLQA, drawn from MC-72 with seed 42 and exposed as `development-audit`. | Concurrency | Engine of 12 lanes (4 shards x 3 lanes) launched with `--max-in-flight 4 --stop-after-timeouts 4`, as MC-91 and MC-92: at most 4 requests in flight, and the run pauses after 4 consecutive timeouts or connection errors with every incomplete item resumable. | Retries | No in-run retry of a failed item; `--resume` re-attempts only items without a completed response under an identical frozen condition. Completed responses, including truncated or unparsed ones, are terminal. Authentication, permission, model-identity and budget errors pause the run. |
| Scoring | B, O and K: frozen V1 scorers on the raw response. S and KS: the answer after the last final-answer label (`diagnostic-final-segment-v1`); a multiple-choice response without the label is unparsed; reading uses the frozen last-label extraction. Unparsed answers stay in denominators; format compliance is reported separately. Reading correctness is exact match. The endpoint reports `completed`/`stop` even at the token cap, so truncation is counted from the completion token count. |
| Gates | Execution requires the `MC-93` heading in `measurement_card.md`, this detail card, the registration with the manifest, protocol, template and evidence hashes above, the endpoint host and model of MC-90, `inference_ready` from the diagnostic readiness report with the controls file, and the router rule and cue lexicon on disk with the SHA-256 values of `registration.router`. |
| Analysis (fixed before data) | Item outcome: strict majority of the 3 replicates. (2) Recovery by hypothesis (`--diagnosis`, plan `diagnostic-prospective-recovery-by-hypothesis-v1`, anchor diagnosis SHA-256 `1df9fc414a90155be48820795e693e20352bada1c1a2d83dd32344984756ed90`): per adjudicated hypothesis the B-incorrect anchors, per-arm rescues and regressions, anchors no arm answers, anchors not reproduced, and contrast candidates that never upgrade a hypothesis by themselves. (3) Router (`analyze_diagnostic_router evaluate`, run once): router, always-B, always-S and the best fixed arm with Wilson intervals and mean reported tokens; exact McNemar of the router against always-B and against always-S with Holm over the two; the deployable ceiling (O left out) next to the B-replicate ceiling; the same comparisons without the 8 gold-not-valid anchors; a descriptive detection block against the anchor diagnosis. The report keys of (2) and (3) are recorded in `registration.json`. |
| Provenance and outputs | `condition.json` with manifest, template, protocol, evidence, retrieval, runner, wrapper, scorer and card hashes, per-attempt usage and response model id, `operator_events.jsonl` for every launch, all under `all_res/diagnostic_prospective/core/qwen3-5-9b-65k-prospective/`; no upload. |
| Comparison limits | n = 60 development anchors, 8 of them with evidence arms: the router test has little power and per-dataset results are descriptive. The anchors were evaluated by earlier runs and audited, so none is pristine held-out; the audited answers are those of MC-72, so an issue may not reproduce. The router's design fit comes from wide items, which share datasets with the anchors. Corpus v0 was assembled from the documents cited for the anchors plus distractors, so K's retrieval is easier than on an open legal corpus. Q4_K_M weights on a non-thinking endpoint; temperature 1.0 makes single draws noisy; the endpoint is shared and may drift. The primary audit was AI-assisted; the 15-item independent subset was graded human-only without the pilot's AI suggestions (self-attested; for one item the reviewer looked up the law with an outside AI). |
