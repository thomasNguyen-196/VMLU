# MC-91 — Qwen3.5-9B-65K (IEC), diagnostic prospective wide supplement: a second B draw on the 1,239 non-anchor items

Registered 2026-10-22 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. The project owner approved the supplement on 2026-10-22 after the MC-90 results were reported. It does not change MC-90: that card's registered text, manifest and analysis stand, and this card only adds a calibration.

| Field | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Why | MC-90 compares one S draw with one B draw per item at temperature 1.0. Its recovery (23.1%) and regression (26.0%) rates in reading therefore mix the effect of S with sampling noise, and its oracle ceiling (61.9% over B and S) cannot be told apart from lucky draws. A second B draw of the same items measures that noise directly. |
| Supplement | `wide-b-r2` in `data/diagnostic_prospective_v1/registration.json`: base phase `wide`, arm B, replicate numbers starting at 2, one replicate. | Analysis (fixed before data) | `noise_reference` in `code_benchmark/analyze_diagnostic_prospective.py`; its result keys are listed in the registration entry and checked by a test. Per family (multiple choice, reading): (1) drift between the two B draws, exact McNemar; (2) B-versus-B recovery (first draw wrong, second right) and regression rate (first right, second wrong) with Wilson intervals, and the agreement rate; (3) S1 against B2 as an independent baseline draw: rescues, regressions, net gain with a passage-cluster bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples, seed 42), exact McNemar; (4) S1 against B2 on the items B1 got wrong and on those it got right, exact McNemar with Holm correction over the four contrasts (two families x two conditions): the selection acts on the first draw only, so regression to the mean cancels in the difference; (5) stable categories (right twice, wrong twice, unstable) and S1 on the items B got wrong twice; (6) oracle ceiling over B1 and S1 next to the ceiling over B1 and B2; (7) mean token-F1 of B1, B2 and S1 for reading; (8) per-dataset values, descriptive only. Parity of B2 per dataset against the MC-83 interval is reported. Reading rule: a second-draw drift (McNemar p < 0.05 in a family, or failed parity) is reported and the noise estimates of that family are labelled run-to-run drift, not pure sampling noise. The registered S-versus-B analysis of MC-90 is neither repeated nor replaced. |

| Comparison limits | Both B draws come from one endpoint at different times and at different concurrency (12-way for part of MC-90, 4-way for this run), so a drift of the endpoint cannot be excluded and is tested; two draws bound the noise but give no per-item reliability; Q4_K_M weights on a non-thinking endpoint; the endpoint reports `completed`/`stop` even when output reaches the token cap, so truncation is read from the completion token count. |
