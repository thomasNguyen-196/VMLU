# MC-116 — MiMo-V2.6-Flash (OpenCode Go) in dsh 0.2.2-rc.1 and omp 18.0.0 over the local folders of legal texts: MC-115's arms DSHF, OMPF and the no-tool baselines (B, C) on the same 646 items (246 MC, 400 closed-book ViBidLQA), two draws, with MiMo in place of Qwen3.5-9B

Registered 2026-11-19 (+07), before any benchmark-item request of this card. The design is in `openspec/changes/diagnostic-legal-agent-mimo`. On 2026-11-19 the owner asked whether to test GLM-5.3-Flash next, then chose MiMo-V2.6-Flash as the second model for the legal agent ("vậy chạy tiếp cho MiMo V2.6 Flash đi"). The owner said MiMo's thinking cannot be turned off, only its level set. A probe then found that on this route the level has no effect either. The owner approved the design ("duyệt"): thinking at the provider's default, a 16,000-token cap per response, MC-112's 65,024-token context window kept, and MC-115's whole design in one card. A 6-attempt smoke test ran with the owner's go ("đồng ý"). This card does not change MC-90 to MC-115: their registered text, manifests, rules, analyses and conclusions stand. Their modules are imported, not edited.
