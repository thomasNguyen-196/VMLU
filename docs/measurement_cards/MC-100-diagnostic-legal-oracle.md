# MC-100 — Qwen3.5-9B-65K (IEC), Legal MC oracle test: B / O on 56 Legal MC items

Registered 2026-10-29 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. The design is `openspec/changes/diagnostic-legal-oracle`. It does not change MC-90 to MC-99: their registered text, manifests, rules and analyses stand.

| Field | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Why | Legal MC items carry a question and four options, no passage. The diagnostic audit judged the input insufficient for 10 of its 12 Legal MC anchors. In MC-93 the cited article alone (arm O) helped on the Legal MC evidence anchors while retrieved articles (arm K) hurt. Before building a legal corpus and testing retrieval on Legal MC, this card measures the ceiling: when Qwen is handed the provision that governs a question, does it fix the questions it answers wrongly, and does it keep the ones it answers rightly? |
| Selection | `data/diagnostic_legal_oracle/protocol.json`. Pool: the 134 Legal MC items (`vi-multimodel-v1-legal-mc-146`) of the wide phase, each answered by arm B on two draws: MC-90 `B-r1` (scores SHA-256 `efdd0240…`) and MC-91 `B-r2` (`e7789648…`); the manifest records both full hashes.<br>• Stratum `wrong`: every item B missed on at least one draw, 28 items (12 missed on both).<br>• Stratum `right`: 28 of the items B answered correctly on both draws, the first 28 in ascending SHA-256 of `legal-oracle-v1|<item_id>`. |

Each saw the exact prompt and the gold letter and returned locations only (page, document, article, clauses). Articles are read as corpus v0 reads them, with two exceptions recorded in the protocol's `reader` and the design: a page that breaks off inside its last article keeps the articles closed before it (NĐ 99/2022), and a Quy chế attached to a Quyết định without article markup is read from the page text after its heading line (QĐ 05/2025/QĐ-TTg, QĐ 21/QĐ-SGDVN).<br>• **Labels.** Each item's provisions are labelled against the gold: `supports_gold`, `contradicts_gold`, `unclear` or `not_found`. The owner also wrote the option the provision makes correct: it equals the gold on 12, and on the other two (LG-0001, LG-0082, both `contradicts_gold`) it is the option the agent had read from the law. Nothing was found for LG-0007 (a Party notice without articles), LG-0024 (the page skips the governing articles) and LG-0070 (a 2025 draft decree not on the site). |

| Analysis (fixed before data) | `analyze_legal_oracle`, plan `diagnostic-legal-oracle-v1`, run once.<br>• **Item score.** The mean correctness of an arm over its two draws (0, 0.5 or 1).<br>• **Primary set.** Items whose final label is `supports_gold`. In each stratum, O against the fresh B draws on the same items: the mean per-item difference with a 95% percentile bootstrap (2,000 resamples, seed 7) and a two-sided paired sign-flip p-value (20,000 resamples, seed 7). Clear rescues (O right on both draws, B wrong on both) and clear regressions are counted.<br>• **Reading rule.** A retrieval study on Legal MC is worth building when O is at least 20 points above B in stratum `wrong` and at most 10 points below B in stratum `right`. Otherwise missing documents are not what Qwen gets wrong on Legal MC, and that line stops.<br>• **Gold check (descriptive).** On the reviewed items, the owner's option against the gold. On the `contradicts_gold` items, each arm's answers that pick the gold's option, the owner's option or another: whether the provision moves Qwen away from a gold the law does not support.<br>• **Description.** The `wrong` items missed on both earlier draws and those missed on one; the same comparison on `contradicts_gold` and `unclear` items; the share of answers that are A per arm; mean reported tokens per arm; evidence length.<br>• The report's top-level keys are recorded in the registration and checked. |

| Comparison limits | • The provisions were located with the gold in view, so O is a ceiling and never deployable: it asks whether Qwen can use the right provision, not whether a retriever can find it.<br>• The items were selected on earlier B draws. Regression to the mean will make some `wrong` items right on a fresh draw; comparing O with fresh B draws on the same items removes that from the difference.<br>• Labels are an agent's judgment except on the 14 reviewed items. The review saw the agents' labels, so its full agreement bounds rather than measures their accuracy.<br>• Gold quality is uneven. Besides the 2 `contradicts_gold` and 3 `unclear` items, the agents' notes flag golds that hold only under a law amended in 2025 (for example LG-0003, LG-0031, LG-0036, LG-0044, LG-0075) and golds that are the closest option but loosely worded. Those items keep `supports_gold` when the cited version supports the gold; the provisions are the versions on the stored pages. The notes are unreviewed and are not counted.<br>• The gold letter is A for 62% of Legal MC items; the share answering A is reported per arm.<br>• With 28 items per stratum, intervals are wide. The reading rule is a decision threshold for the next step, not a significance claim.<br>• Provisions are verbatim and keep page artifacts: amendment markers, footnote numbers, a chapter heading at the end of BLDS 2015 Điều 569, and run-together points in NĐ 153/2020 Điều 9.<br>• Single draws at temperature 1.0 are noisy; two draws per arm bound this.<br>• The endpoint may have drifted since MC-96; the model-id check and B, which repeats the MC-90 and MC-91 condition, can reveal it. |
