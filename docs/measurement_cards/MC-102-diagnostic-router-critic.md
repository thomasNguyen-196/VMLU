# MC-102 — Muse Spark 1.3 (OpenCode Go), router-critic design check: judging Qwen3.5-9B's logged reading answers

Registered 2026-10-30 (+07) before benchmark-item requests of this card. The design is `openspec/changes/diagnostic-router-loop` (tasks 1.1, 1.6 and the Phase 3 design check). It does not change MC-90 to MC-101: their registered text, manifests, rules and analyses stand.

| Field | Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Why | **The owner's goal.** On 2026-10-30 the owner asked for a router that reads the student's answer, judges whether and why it is weak, and applies a fix. **What the logged runs already show** (exploratory, `simulate_router_loop`):<br>• On reading items, a loop that asks B twice and switches to S when the two answers differ stays well below the per-item bound.<br>• Detecting a wrong answer by comparing exact wording with the stronger model's logged answer flags many correct answers: 91 of 359 on Vi-SQuAD.<br>• On multiple choice, the logged answers of Muse already settle the question: judging an answer is solving the item, and Muse alone scores higher.<br>**What this card asks.** The critic adds new information mainly as a meaning-level judge of free-text answers and as a chooser among fixes. This card tests that once, cheaply: Muse judges Qwen's logged first answer, and the loop is replayed with Qwen's logged answers. |
| Critic | `data/diagnostic_router_loop/critic_v1.json`, SHA-256 `a08b5dadbd94bdaa13068efab27abccc35550940f2c807b3cad55349d3f9e8da` (`muse-critic-reading-v1`).<br>• **Input.** One Vietnamese prompt per item: the passage and the question of the item's frozen reading prompt, and Qwen's answer.<br>• **Task.** Judge the answer by meaning, then choose one fix: `giu` (keep, B), `suy_luan` (reason step by step, S) or `trich_dan` (quote then answer, Q).<br>• **Output.** Three closed last lines: `Đánh giá` (`dung`, `sai`, `khong_chac`), `Nguyên nhân` (`tinh_toan`, `tim_sai_cho`, `dien_dat`, `khac`, `khong_co`) and `Cách sửa`. Labels are parsed with case, accents and markup ignored. An id outside its list, or a missing label, is unrecognised.<br>• The critic never sees the gold or a score, and nothing it writes reaches the student. |
| Items | The 376 Vi-DROP (188) and Vi-SQuAD (188) items of the menu set (MC-97; menu manifest self-hash `993694c4…`). The judged answer is the scored prediction of Qwen's B-r1 attempt (MC-90 scores, SHA-256 `efdd024049e0e26d44e23dc9ff014032f3cfaa7e5517c500aef9d7c5a18a4a26`). There is one critic call per item, with run key `dataset:item:critic-1`. Manifest `critic_manifest_v1.json`, self-hash `8a13231a6b3e6e10cb72863e50db562b6d8e040cb9bffffc50ebbacdf0f2dd65`. |
| Replay | No student request is sent. Every student answer is a logged draw-1 attempt of Qwen3.5-9B: B (MC-90), S (MC-90) and Q (MC-97).<br>• **Critic loop.** On a `sai` verdict, take the prescribed arm's answer (`giu` → B-r1, `suy_luan` → S-r1, `trich_dan` → Q-r1). Otherwise keep B-r1. An unrecognised output keeps B-r1.<br>• **Rule loop**, the comparator. Keep B-r1 when it agrees with B-r2 under `normalized-answer-v1`; otherwise take S-r1. |
| Model / route | Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor (`muse-spark-1.3-contributor`) on the direct OpenCode Go Responses API, host `opencode.ai`, as MC-98, non-streaming. The credential comes from the local OpenCode auth store and is never written to outputs. Temperature and reasoning effort are omitted (provider defaults). `max_output_tokens=4096`; 300 s timeout; no SDK retries; session header per item. |
| Limits | At most 4 requests in flight; a breaker after 4 consecutive network failures; a budget of 2,000,000 reported tokens (expected about 1.2 million). The run halts on an authentication error or a changed response model. Completed responses, including truncated or unrecognised ones, are terminal; `--resume` re-sends only items without a completed response. |
| Gates | Execution requires all of: the `MC-102` heading in `measurement_card.md`; this detail card; `data/diagnostic_router_loop/registration.json` with the critic-configuration and manifest SHA-256 above; and the route resolving to the registered host and model. The runner rebuilds the manifest, including every judged answer, and refuses to run when it differs. |
| Analysis (fixed before data) | `analyze_router_critic`, plan `diagnostic-router-critic-v1`, run once.<br>• **Metric.** The token-F1 of the selected draw-1 answer; exact match is reported beside it.<br>• **Primary.** The critic loop against the rule loop over the 376 items: the mean per-item difference with a percentile bootstrap (2,000 resamples, seed 7) and a two-sided sign-flip p-value (20,000 resamples, seed 7).<br>• **Reading rule.** The critic goes on to held-out reading items when it beats the rule loop by at least 1.0 token-F1 point with p < 0.05. Otherwise the reading critic stops at the design stage.<br>• **Description.**<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– Per dataset: always-B, always-S, always-Q, the rule loop, the critic loop, the variant that treats `khong_chac` as `sai`, Muse alone (MC-80/81 logged answers), the proxy "Muse's logged answer differs from B-r1 → S-r1", and the per-item bound over B, S and Q.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– The critic's verdict against B-r1's correctness (token-F1 ≥ 0.5), with recall and precision.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– Each prescription's rescue and damage counts.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– Verdict, cause and fix distributions, format compliance and truncation.<br>&nbsp;&nbsp;– Muse and Qwen tokens per item.<br>• The report's top-level keys are recorded in the registration and checked. |
| Provenance and outputs | `condition.json` holds the critic configuration, manifest, judged scores, menu manifest, module, runner and card hashes, the route and the limits. Also kept: per-attempt usage, the raw response and response model id, `critic.jsonl` (the parsed fields) and `operator_events.jsonl`. All sit under `all_res/diagnostic_router_loop/critic/muse-spark-1-3-contributor/`; no upload. |
| Comparison limits | • **Not held-out.** A design-stage check on items that earlier routers used for design: MC-90 to MC-92 and MC-97.<br>• **Replay.** The loop picks among logged answers, so no answer a real second round would produce is measured, and the critic's prescriptions cannot use a fix outside B, S and Q.<br>• **Scoring mismatch.** The critic judges meaning, but scores are token-F1 and exact match. A critic that rightly keeps a correct paraphrase gains nothing under token-F1, and one that rightly rejects a fluent wrong answer is credited only if the fix scores better.<br>• **One call per item.** The critic's consistency is not measured. Muse's temperature and reasoning settings are the provider's and cannot be observed.<br>• **Muse alone** comes from MC-80/81, a different date and route setting, and is a reference, not a matched arm. |
